About

A generalist at heart, a fiduciary by trade.

I live in Madras / Chennai — a southern Indian port city known for its kutcheris, its beaches, and its thayir sadam. For three decades I have made allocation, risk-taking, and position-sizing decisions for myself and my family, and today I put that experience to work for a handful of clients.

Away from the markets I shoot street, abstract, and portrait photography, make vector and raster art, and read like a self-confessed book snob. I play squash, golf, and billiards, lift through CrossFit HIIT, and lose the odd evening to tabletop games.

I am an advocate for accessibility rights, particularly for the Deaf and hard-of-hearing — a value I try to carry into everything I build, including this website.
